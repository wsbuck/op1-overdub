op1-overdub — original OP-1, firmware 246

New synth engines, FX with animated screens, and keyboard modes for the original OP-1.

They are patched into TE's own firmware: every stock engine, FX and feature keeps working. Like an overdub on tape, it adds new layers over TE's firmware without erasing anything underneath.

Not affiliated with or supported by teenage engineering. You flash this at your own risk (see safety).

keyboard
  • HOLD — SHIFT + T2 on the synth screen latches released notes; again to release.
  • CHORD — hold SHIFT and play a chord (up to 4 notes); then every key plays that shape. SHIFT + one key turns it off.
install
  1. Download stock firmware 246 from TE: op1_246.op1.
  2. Download overdub-*.zip from releases and unzip it.
  3. python3 apply.py op1_246.op1 (Python 3.10+, no other dependencies) writes OVERDUB.OP1. It checks that your file is stock 246 and that the result is exactly the expected image.
  4. On the OP-1: hold COM while switching on, press 1 (upload firmware), connect USB, copy OVERDUB.OP1 to the OP-1 disk, eject. Same procedure as an official update.

The release contains only our code, our database rows (engine/FX names and presets) and our SVG screens. TE's firmware never leaves your computer.

safety
  • The bootloader is never touched. te-boot.ldr stays byte-identical to stock (the tools refuse otherwise), so you can always go back: hold COM at power-on, upload stock op1_246.op1.
  • Before going back to stock, delete sounds saved with these engines or FX. Stock firmware doesn't know their names and stops at boot on such a sound. If that happens: COM at power-on, then factory reset.
  • Never name a firmware file with FRC, RBT or KEY in it: the OP-1's updater treats those as commands. apply.py and build.py refuse such names.
